# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before making assumptions.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus on the work, evidence, and user impact.
- Protect private, confidential, and personally identifying information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or take other action when conduct violates this policy.
